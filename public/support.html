Noteblock — Support

We're here to help.

Contact us

Email support@mynoteblock.com and we'll get back to you. We aim to respond within a couple of days, and to reports of objectionable content within 24 hours.

Report content or a user

Tap the ••• menu on any review, post, comment, mix, guestbook entry, message, or profile and choose Report. Reports go to our moderation team; we remove content and ban users that violate our Terms within 24 hours.

Block someone

Open the person's profile and choose Block. You won't see each other's posts, comments, or messages. You can unblock from the same profile at any time.

Delete your account

Go to Settings → delete account. This permanently removes your account and your content.

Trouble signing in

Use the password-reset option on the sign-in screen to get a reset code by email. If you don't receive it, check your spam folder or email support@mynoteblock.com.

More

See our Privacy Policy. The Terms of Use and Privacy Policy are also available in the app under Settings.
